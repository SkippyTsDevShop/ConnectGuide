What Is Learn2Earn?

Last updated: September 2026

Definition The 4 steps What you may receive Who is eligible What it is not Sources

For the full walkthrough, see the Learn2Earn complete guide.

Short definition

Learn2Earn (L2E) is Connect United's rewarded education program. Eligible members complete a weekly lesson cycle — learn, quiz, action step, verification — and can receive blockchain-based rewards such as tokens and/or NFTs for verified participation.

It is Proof of Action applied to education: you do not get credit for only watching. You demonstrate understanding and complete a required action.

The 4 steps (from Connect support)

Each week, a new lesson is posted in the Connect platform's Weekly Learn2Earn section. To qualify during the live weekly window, eligible users must complete all four steps:

  1. Watch the lesson video or read the lesson content
  2. Take the short quiz to prove you understand the core ideas
  3. Complete the action step — a simple, real-world application of the lesson
  4. Submit the verification form (use your Connect email; spelling mistakes can delay rewards)

What you may receive

Connect's Learn2Earn article describes rewards such as:

  • NFTs — unique digital assets related to the lesson
  • Tokens — digital rewards such as WIN, earned through proof of learning and action

Rewards are described as going to your Connect wallet; visibility may take time depending on blockchain activity. Support materials have noted NFTs issuing on a weekly schedule (for example, Wednesdays) — confirm current timing in the live platform/support docs.

Who is eligible?

Access to Learn2Earn reward eligibility is tied to products described in support docs, including:

  • Owned BlockBot — described as lifetime Learn2Earn reward access
  • Weekly Pass NFT — reward access for one week
  • Yearly Pass NFT — reward access for 52 weeks

Always confirm current eligibility rules on support.connectunited.com before assuming access.

What Learn2Earn is not

  • It is not a guaranteed-income program
  • It is not the same as the Blockchain Academy (Academy = self-paced learning library; Learn2Earn = weekly rewarded cycle)
  • It is not financial advice
