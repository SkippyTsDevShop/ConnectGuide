The short answer: No, it is not a scam

Connect United operates on the WIN Blockchain — a live, community-owned Layer-1 network that has been producing blocks since launch. The blockchain is independently verifiable by anyone, at any time, without creating an account or contacting Connect United.

Scams do not have independently auditable blockchains, publicly documented governance frameworks, multi-year operational track records, or transparent token economics. Connect United has all of these. That does not make it risk-free — no blockchain project is — but it means the basic legitimacy questions have verifiable answers.

This page is for skeptics. Everything here links to primary sources — blockchain explorers, third-party review platforms, and publicly available governance documents that Connect United does not control. Also see the Connect United DYOR guide and the official Connect Reference Guide.

Age and longevity: fly-by-night operations do not last five years

Most crypto scams are designed for a short run. They do not build multi-year operational track records or maintain public blockchain explorers. Longevity is one of the clearest anti-scam signals available.

How to verify Connect United's age independently:

  • Blockchain genesis. win.elevatescan.com shows block production history from genesis — first blocks produced in 2021.
  • Domain age. Scamadviser's first analysis of connectunited.com is dated October 2021. Scam operations rarely survive long enough to accumulate years of third-party analysis.
  • Continuous on-chain activity. 1.88 million blocks does not happen overnight. The explorer shows consistent activity across years.
  • Published track record. After one year of Learn2Earn, Connect United published a detailed breakdown of 9,000 participants and 300,000 lessons — read it with on-chain references you can verify.

Your assets: Connect United cannot access your wallet

WIN tokens — whether Layer-1 or ERC-20 — are held in non-custodial wallets. That means:

  • Your private key controls your wallet. Connect United does not hold your key.
  • Connect United cannot access, move, freeze, or confiscate your tokens.
  • If Connect United ceased to exist tomorrow, your on-chain assets would remain in your wallet.
  • NFT credentials from Learn2Earn belong to your wallet address — not to a Connect United account.

The flip side: if you lose your seed phrase, no one can recover your wallet — not Connect United, not anyone. See the non-custodial wallet guide →

Real risks to understand — even if it is not a scam

  • Token price volatility. WIN token value fluctuates with broader crypto market conditions.
  • Nodes must be online to earn. An offline node generates no rewards. You need to self-host or use Codex.
  • Governance risk. Node owner votes could change blockchain distribution parameters. Decentralized governance means collective decisions that may not favor every participant equally.
  • Regulatory uncertainty. Crypto regulation varies by jurisdiction and is actively evolving.
  • Liquidity risk. WIN may have lower liquidity than major cryptocurrencies.
